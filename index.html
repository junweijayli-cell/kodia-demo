KORDIASignature.

A single designed programme — teak, rope and cast aluminium drawn as one family, so a terrace reads as one idea rather than six catalogue pages.

Why it exists

One family, not a catalogue.

The trade catalogue answers "what do you make?". Signature answers "what would you specify?". Every piece shares a frame language, a rope gauge and a cushion tone, so an importer can furnish a whole space from one page and know it will arrive matching.

Signature imagery is computer-rendered. Production samples are photographed on request — ask us before you specify a finish.

    A furniture factory, not a trading office.

    KORDIA manufactures in Chancheng District, Foshan — the centre of China's outdoor furniture industry. Production covers rope weaving, aluminium tube fabrication, cast-aluminium casting, powder coating, cushion sewing and final assembly under one roof, which is why a custom colourway is quoted in 48 hours instead of 4 weeks.

    We supply importers, hospitality groups and specifiers across Europe, the Middle East, North America and Australia.
